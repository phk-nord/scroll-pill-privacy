Scroll Pill

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the Scroll Pill browser extension, version 1.0.0 and later

Scroll Pill adds a floating control that jumps to the top or bottom of a page. This policy explains what the extension reads, where anything goes, and the choices you have.

In short: Scroll Pill does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data. It makes no network requests at all.

Everything happens locally in your browser. Your settings stay in your browser profile, and there is no developer server, telemetry, analytics, or advertising.

What Scroll Pill handles

Page structure and scroll state

To decide what to scroll and where to place the pill, the extension reads the current page locally: scroll positions, which elements can scroll, element tags, ARIA roles, ids and class names, element positions and sizes, and a small amount of computed style. This information is used only in the page you are viewing and is discarded when the page closes. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

Site colors (optional)

If you choose the Website colors design — or Material You without a tint — the extension reads the page's declared theme-color or works out its most prominent color from header, navigation, button, and link styles, so the pill can match the site. This is computed locally and is not retained.

Blocked sites

The extension compares the current page's hostname with your own blocklist so the pill can stay hidden on sites you choose. The hostname is checked in memory; it is not added to a history or sent anywhere.

Keyboard and pointer events

Scroll Pill listens for the Shift and Escape keys, and for recent wheel, touch, key, and pointer activity, to support dragging, collapsing, and deciding which container you most recently scrolled. It does not log, record, or transmit keystrokes or pointer events.

Settings

Your preferences are stored with chrome.storage.sync inside your browser profile: whether the pill is shown, pill and settings-page themes, the dark-mode schedule times, the design and tint, idle transparency, position and free-floating coordinates, the collapse breakpoint, the smooth-scrolling choice, and your blocked-site list.

No browsing history or page content

Scroll Pill does not keep a history of pages, URLs, screenshots, text, form values, or scroll positions. It does not read cookies, passwords, clipboard contents, or autofill data.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Scroll Pill has no backend and makes no network requests. It does not load remote scripts, styles, fonts, images, or advertising. All code ships in the extension package.

Browser sync. chrome.storage.sync is provided by your browser. If you enable Chrome Sync, Chrome may store your settings in your Google account under Google's terms. The developer has no access to that data and cannot retrieve or delete it.

No third parties. There is no analytics provider, crash reporter, ad network, or data broker involved. The only external link in the extension is the privacy-policy link on the settings page, which opens only when you click it.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Storage — saves the settings described above so they survive restarts and, if you use Chrome Sync, follow your browser profile.
  • Read and change all your data on all websites — the pill has to be available on whatever page you are reading, so the content script runs on all sites. Its access is limited to the local page signals described above; it does not modify page content.

Scroll Pill requests no other permissions. It cannot access your tabs list, downloads, bookmarks, clipboard, microphone, camera, or location.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or receive any data from the extension.
  • We do not use telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or remote assets.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share extension data with anyone.
  • We do not use data for personalized or retargeted advertising.
  • We do not read cookies, form fields, clipboard contents, or keystrokes.
  • We do not build a browsing history or profile of you.
  • We do not allow humans to read user data, because we never receive any.

Retention and deletion

Settings remain in chrome.storage.sync until you change them, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall Scroll Pill. Removing the extension removes its local data from your browser profile.

If Chrome Sync is enabled, a copy may remain in your Google account until it is removed through your Google sync settings. Because we hold no server-side copy of any data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Scroll Pill keeps its state in browser-provided storage and makes no network requests, so there is no transmission to intercept. The pill is rendered in a closed shadow root, which keeps page styles and the control isolated from each other. No method of local storage is completely secure; protect your browser profile and device.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Scroll Pill uses information only to provide its single purpose: offering one-click navigation to the top or bottom of a page. Its handling of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell user data, use it for personalized advertising, or allow humans to read it.

This website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page is static, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log visitor IP addresses for security and abuse prevention, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. The Scroll Pill extension itself does not contact this website or GitHub.

Children

Scroll Pill is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the extension sends us no data at all.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Scroll Pill changes. The effective date at the top of this page will change, and the revision history for this public repository records each version. Continued use of Scroll Pill after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, open an issue at github.com/phk-nord/scroll-pill-privacy/issues. GitHub issues are public, so please do not include sensitive personal data.
